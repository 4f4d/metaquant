# META QUANT — Glossary

## Arbitrage
A strategy that seeks to exploit a price relationship whose economics imply a constrained or hedged opportunity, subject to costs and execution risk.

## Basis
The relationship between a spot instrument and its futures contract. A basis strategy studies deviations from an economically defined fair relationship.

## Cointegration
A statistical relationship in which a combination of individually non-stationary series can be stationary. It is one possible foundation for statistical arbitrage.

## Spread
A defined difference or residual between related instruments. The exact definition depends on the strategy.

## Z-score
A standardized measure of how far an observation is from a reference mean in standard-deviation units.

## Market impact
The price effect caused by executing an order or trading a meaningful amount of available liquidity.

## Slippage
The difference between an assumed/desired execution price and the price actually modeled/received.

## Legging risk
Risk that one leg of a multi-leg trade fills while the other does not fill as intended.

## Paper trading
Real/near-real market observations with simulated orders and simulated fills.

## Shadow trading
A live-like decision/routing process whose orders are recorded but not sent to the market.

## Walk-forward validation
Repeated train/validate/test evaluation where later test data are kept unseen during model development.

## Look-ahead bias
Using information in a backtest that would not have been available when the decision was made.

## Market regime
A state describing changing market conditions, such as normal trading, closing auction, high-volatility shock, or liquidity stress.

## Laya
An open-source System-1 typed decision model considered by META QUANT for semantic context tasks such as news/policy-event classification. It is not a trading-risk authority.
